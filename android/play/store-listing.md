## English

**App name** (max 30 characters)

```
IonoProp – HF band advisor
```

**Short description** (max 80 characters)

```
Ionosphere map and HF band advice for radio amateurs, checked with real spots
```

**Full description** (max 4000 characters)

```
Which band should you use right now, and in which direction? IonoProp tells you.

The app works from live measurements: the world's ionosondes (KC2G / GIRO),
solar flux and geomagnetic activity (NOAA SWPC). From these it models the
ionosphere above your QTH and anywhere in the world, and shows band by band
where your signal can reach.

FEATURES
• Band advice from 160 m to 6 m (including 8 m) for 15 target areas (Europe, USA, Japan,
  Australia, South America…) – green, yellow, orange, dark: good, fair,
  poor, closed.
• World map: MUF and foF2 maps up to 50 MHz – high low-VHF F2 MUFs
  show up too, or pick a band to see where your signal
  reaches on it. Day/night shading, ionosondes, the path with its hops.
• 24-hour forecast for any target area.
• Time slider: see what conditions will be like 1–23 hours ahead.
• Real spots from PSK Reporter: see where signals from your area actually
  got in the last 30 minutes – marked as strong (SSB-copyable) or weak
  only (FT8). You see at once whether the prediction matches reality.
• Warnings for solar flares and auroral disturbance.
• Tap anywhere on the map to set a new target.
• Your own targets by locator, callsign or prefix; long path.
• SSB, CW or FT8: weak-signal modes keep more bands open.
• Sporadic E from real spots and the nearest ionosonde.
• Notifications on band openings: Es on 10/8/6 m, F2 on 8/6 m, or a band
  opening towards your own targets.
• Home screen widget: solar activity and band advice at a glance.
• Grey line, sunrise/sunset, Kp forecast.
• English and Hungarian user interface.

WHO IS IT FOR?
Radio amateurs who want to know which band is open now and where to look
for DX.

GOOD TO KNOW
• This is a model, not a guarantee. It is most accurate in Europe, where
  many ionosondes measure.
• It does not forecast sporadic E or solar flares; it shows openings as
  they happen.
• Free, no ads, no registration.
• Your locator stays on your phone.

Data sources: KC2G (prop.kc2g.com), GIRO ionosonde network, NOAA SWPC,
PSK Reporter, Natural Earth. Many thanks to them!

73!
```

|---|---|
